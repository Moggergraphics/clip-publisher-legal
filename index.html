About Clip Publisher

Clip Publisher is a desktop creator utility used by Moggergraphics to organize, caption, and publish original short-form gaming videos across connected social media channels.

  • Runs locally on the creator's desktop workstation
  • Publishes original creator-approved short-form video clips
  • Uses official platform OAuth 2.0 authorization with user-controlled tokens
