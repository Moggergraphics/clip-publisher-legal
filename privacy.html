Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Clip Publisher (Moggergraphics)

Home Terms of Service Privacy Policy

1. Information We Access

Clip Publisher runs locally on the creator's own desktop computer and accesses only the minimum OAuth 2.0 permissions explicitly approved by the user (such as basic profile identifier and video upload permissions) to publish user-selected video files.

2. Local Storage & No Third-Party Sharing

OAuth credentials, access tokens, and video files are stored strictly locally on the user's own machine. Clip Publisher does not operate external tracking servers and does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

3. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You can revoke Clip Publisher's access at any time from your TikTok, Google, or Meta account security settings, and delete local tokens directly from your device.

4. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed via GitHub (Moggergraphics).
